About this chart

The original HD artwork is the geographic background. Pins describe their own evidence and coordinate frame. Regional anchors are approximate; political-map pins identify printed symbols. Decorative castles do not identify settlements.

No surveyed distance scale is established. Blank route or history layers mean no approved records, not that the world has no roads or history.

Player knowledge and visits are campaign records. A browser bookmark does not discover a location.
